# Decision log

Decisions from the owner interview on 2026-10-08. Each entry records the decision and the reason. Later decisions that change an entry add a new entry rather than editing the old one. Plan and gates: [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).

## Purpose and setting

- **D1 · Use:** VJ for other people's DJ sets. _Why:_ this is the first real use; tempo, track changes and screens are not under the owner's control.
- **D2 · Control mix:** improvise, play prepared material and run hands-off, with hands-off carrying most of a night. _Why:_ nobody fires clips from a trackpad for eight hours.
- **D3 · First show:** 1–3 months; planning date 2026-12-17 until a real date exists, freeze two weeks before. **Date fixed, scope flexes.** _Why:_ gig dates do not move, and a few excellent families beat many unfinished ones.
- **D4 · Night length:** 4–8 hours. _Why:_ sets the endurance gate at eight hours unattended.
- **D5 · Hardware:** laptop only, no MIDI controller; MIDI learn stays for later. _Why:_ owner's choice; the keyboard becomes the controller.

## Output and platform

- **D6 · Output:** HDMI straight to the screen; Phosphor is the whole show; browser-only (Chrome). _Why:_ no Syphon/Spout/NDI needed, so no native wrapper.
- **D7 · Screens:** any aspect ratio. Math and raymarch families compose natively; simulation families cover-crop from a fixed grid. Quality tiers become a ≈2.1 MP pixel budget. A framing test pattern helps the line check. _Why:_ club LED walls are rarely 16:9.
- **D8 · Output window owns the show:** it renders at native resolution and owns show state, audio input, beat tracking and autopilot; the control window is a remote with a preview. _Why:_ no stream delay on blackout, paced by the projector's 60 Hz instead of the laptop's 120 Hz, and a control-window crash or reload leaves the picture untouched.
- **D9 · Crash recovery:** grid, current clip, tempo and shared controls restore automatically; back on screen in ≤ 10 s with ≤ 2 clicks (Chrome's window-management API places the output). Autosave on edits only, never during playback. _Why:_ a 3 a.m. reload must not end the night; playback is not an edit.
- **D10 · Preflight:** before Show mode, the app checks input level, beat lock, output screen, framing pattern and wake lock; a manual list covers mains power, Do Not Disturb, sleep and Chrome's "Warn before quitting". _Why:_ a page cannot block Cmd+Q.

## Playing

- **D11 · Clip grid** instead of a linear score. _Why:_ nobody can pre-compose cues for a DJ set they don't control.
- **D12 · Linear score retired:** cue timeline, keyframes and MIDI transport are removed; old v2 cues convert to grid clips. _Why:_ less surface; the non-finite keyframe bug lives there.
- **D13 · Grid shape:** 4×8 per page on keyboard rows 1–8, Q–I, A–K and Y/Z–comma by physical position, eight pages, triggers quantized to the beat by default (or bar, or immediate), per-clip fade. _Why:_ the keyboard is the controller.
- **D14 · Key map:** Shift+1–8 pages; Esc blackout; Shift+Esc safe look; Space tap; Enter downbeat; ←/→ beat nudge; ↑/↓ energy; 9 half speed, 0 double speed (again for normal); P autopilot; O freeze; hold L flash. The original Show/Prep button and confirmation are superseded by D52. Labels follow the active keyboard layout. _Why:_ panic and tempo stay off the grid keys.
- **D15 · Autopilot is the default:** plays autopilot-allowed clips on the current page, changes every 32 bars (16/32/64) on a bar line, avoids the last six clips; on a build raises energy, on a drop cuts to a higher-energy clip on the downbeat, on a breakdown lowers energy and halves speed, then switches to a calmer clip after eight bars. It may change clip, energy, speed trim and mirror, never master, blackout, flash or page. Manual input takes over; control returns after 32 idle bars. _Why:_ pages become the owner's steering wheel for the night.
- **D16 · Show and Prep modes.** Superseded by D52. _Why:_ a stray click in Show mode must not wreck a look.

## Controls

- **D17 · Shared controls:** master, energy, speed trim, hue, zoom, mirror, flash. Energy replaces audio-reactivity amount. _Why:_ the same keys and faders mean the same thing in every family.
- **D18 · Family controls:** three live faders per family and all parameters in the editor (available in the unified UI under D52).
- **D19 · What a clip stores:** family, all parameters, seed, palette, base energy, fade, quantize, autopilot flag, thumbnail. Shared controls are not stored. _Why:_ shared controls behave like a mixer strip.
- **D20 · Manual audio routing removed.** _Why:_ family energy curves and shared beat responses define how each family reacts.

## Timing

- **D21 · Audio source:** design for a line feed from the DJ mixer via a USB interface; the microphone is a fallback where tap carries sync. _Why:_ beat tracking through a mic in a loud room is unreliable.
- **D22 · Beat detection scope:** automatic tempo and beat phase; downbeat by key; builds, drops and breakdowns from loudness; no automatic phrase detection in v1. Techno/house, 4/4, 100–150 BPM with half/double folding. _Why:_ downbeat and phrase detection are hard; 4/4 club music is the tractable case.
- **D23 · Own beat tracker:** low-band onset detection, tempo estimate, phase lock and coasting through breakdowns, in an AudioWorklet. _Why:_ essentia.js (AGPL) and aubio (GPL) conflict with the MIT license; small MIT libraries estimate tempo without phase.
- **D24 · Manual path is required:** tap, downbeat and nudge must fully work alone; auto tracking ships only if it meets its targets on the owner's mixes. _Why:_ the show cannot depend on the riskiest feature.

## Visuals

- **D25 · Generative only:** no logos, text or video. _Why:_ stays out of general VJ-software scope.
- **D26 · Energy range:** every family except Aquarium reaches from very low to very high energy. _Why:_ owner requirement for nights that move between warm-up and peak time.
- **D27 · Energy fader:** each family defines an energy curve; clips store a base energy; owner and autopilot push it live. Energy moves motion speed, beat response, density/detail and contrast; brightness stays on master; flashes unlock only in the top ~20 % and go through the limiter. Acceptance: an energy ladder at 0.1 / 0.5 / 0.9 the owner orders correctly at a glance. _Why:_ drops can be played inside a clip, not only by switching clips.
- **D28 · Shared beat responses:** kick-driven camera punch, zoom kick, palette pulse and injection burst live in the engine; each family declares which it supports; energy scales their strength. _Why:_ only Interference and Tapestry respond to the beat today.
- **D29 · Flashes:** a shared flash control behind a limiter of about three flashes per second, on by default and locked during a show. _Why:_ beat-synced flashes are a club staple; photosensitivity risk is real.
- **D30 · Types:** a type is a structurally different version of a family (geometry, camera, motion grammar or layout), not a palette or parameter tweak. Three to four types per family, each spanning the full energy range. _Why:_ owner requirement: types within a family must be more distinct and diverse.
- **D31 · Distinctness check:** per-family sheet of all types at the same palette and mid energy, rendered in grayscale; a structural metric (spatial frequency, edge density, symmetry, motion) flags near pairs, also across families; owner signs off. _Why:_ colour must not fake difference.
- **D32 · Families:** 16 in total. Kept: Cathedral, Feedback, Interference, Tapestry, Melt, Acid. Acid absorbs Evolution Garden as a "garden" type; breeding stays as the general prep tool. Magnetic and Phase are reworked and return. Aquarium returns as the only calm-only family. New: v6 ports Fractal Flight, Julia Observatory, Fourth Dimension, Hyperbolic Loom; plus Pulse Geometry, Particle Swarm, Light Beams. Oscilloscope and Terrain are optional. _Why:_ owner asked for all missing families; club gaps are hard-edged geometry, particles, beams and flythroughs.
- **D33 · Port approach:** v6 families are rebuilt as GPU scenes on contract v3, porting the idea rather than Canvas2D code. Fractal Flight's manual flying becomes authored camera paths, one per type. _Why:_ WASD clashes with the keyboard grid; autopilot needs autonomous motion.
- **D34 · Authoring:** Claude breeds candidates along the energy axis and renders contact sheets; the owner picks and fine-tunes. _Why:_ Claude does the search, the owner makes the artistic calls.

## Scope and process

- **D35 · Cut:** linear score and keyframes, WebM and frame-sequence export (PNG snapshots stay), MIDI clock, manual audio routing, Safari and Firefox, Milestone 3, further demo work. _Why:_ none serves VJing other people's sets.
- **D36 · Required for the first show:** output-window renderer, keyboard grid, Show/Prep, tap/downbeat/nudge, energy fader and beat responses, flash limiter, crash recovery, preflight, telemetry, cover-crop for any screen, bar-count autopilot; at least six families through the gate, two strong at peak time. Auto beat tracking, build/drop autopilot and native odd-screen composition ship only if ready.
- **D37 · Family order:** (1) Cathedral, Interference, Feedback, Tapestry; (2) Pulse Geometry, Fractal Flight; (3) Melt, Acid; (4) Light Beams, Particle Swarm; (5) Julia, Fourth Dimension, Hyperbolic Loom; (6) Magnetic and Phase reworks, Aquarium. _Why:_ peak-time value first, then readiness.
- **D38 · Gates:** family gate and show gate as defined in the roadmap. _Why:_ explicit, measurable definitions of done.
- **D39 · Codebase:** keep `engine.mjs`, scene files and set validation; replace `app.js` with tested modules (show state, beat tracker, autopilot, output renderer, control UI) rather than refactoring in place. _Why:_ much of `app.js` serves cut features.
- **D40 · Unmerged lanes:** review and merge `lane/test-harness`, then `lane/image-pipeline`, then `lane/app-core`, and build on top; score/transport parts are removed later with the score. _Why:_ they already contain the flash limiter, contact sheets, soak runner, telemetry and live-show bug fixes.
- **D41 · Process:** platform changes one at a time on `main`; after contract v3, families in parallel branches, each merged only after passing its gate. CI covers unit tests, synthetic beat tests and headless render smoke; mixes, soaks and sign-off stay local. _Why:_ platform contracts must settle before families build on them.
- **D42 · Schedule:** as in the roadmap, with a checkpoint on 2026-11-11 (fewer than three group 1 families passing → group 2 leaves first-show scope; Melt and Acid move up).
- **D43 · v6 set import:** only if the owner has v6 sets worth keeping; otherwise skipped.

## Follow-up interview, 2026-10-08

- **D44 · Flash-limiter cost:** the earlier 0.3 ms/frame target is dropped; the family gate's frame p99 ≤ 34 ms on the rig is the binding limit, and the limiter is optimized only if a family misses it. _Why:_ the limiter costs 0.8–1.0 ms but every family still renders in ≤ ~6 ms; the gate measures what the audience sees.
- **D45 · Governor order:** a raymarched family lowers its ray steps (96 → 64 → 40, per family) before the pixel budget drops; both count as downgrades under the show gate. _Why:_ fewer steps are less visible on an LED wall than a softer picture.
- **D46 · Failed families are skipped:** a family whose shaders fail is never scheduled again and is cut away from if on screen; the show keeps playing. _Why:_ one driver or shader fault must not end the night.
- **D47 · Ungated families stay as drafts:** families committed before passing the gate stay in the tree; gate status is tracked in the roadmap and only gated families go into the show set. _Why:_ status decides what plays, not branch location; no branch churn.
- **D48 · Family lanes:** group 1 runs as four parallel lanes (one branch and worktree per family); a lane may rebuild failing types until at least three pass, then sends the owner a sign-off packet; group 2 follows in queue order, at most five lanes at once. Real-GPU timing runs one at a time on the rig. _Why:_ families are independent once contract v3 is on `main`; GPU timing is only valid without competing load.
- **D49 · Default mode plays at once:** a fresh stage starts the demo beat at 120 BPM (clock default 120), autopilot on; the chosen audio source survives a reload. A new set holds every family on page 1. _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-08: opening Phosphor should already show a pulsing, changing picture.
- **D50 · Random mode, always smooth:** a Random switch (default on) makes autopilot pick clips at random and glide the live clip's continuous parameters around their saved values; off, it walks the page in order without drift. Autopilot changes always crossfade (two bars; drops one bar) and no longer jump the mirror. Supersedes the "cut" on drops and the mirror permission in D15. _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-08: switching and parameter changes must be smooth always and random by default.
- **D51 · Groups 1 and 2 approved:** the owner signed off all six packets (Feedback, Tapestry, Cathedral, Interference, Pulse Geometry, Fractal Flight), including the flagged cross-family near pairs; with rig timing passed, all six pass the family gate. _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-08 ("approve them all"); six families is the first-show minimum.
- **D52 · Unified controls, optional settings lock:** all performance controls work on the local preview without a stage; configuration remains editable while the stage is open, and grid clicks also trigger the live output. Replace the mandatory Show/Prep toggle with a top-bar **Lock settings** switch, optional and enabled only while a stage is connected. It disables clip, set, audio and MIDI configuration while keeping grid/performance controls usable. Supersedes D16 and the mode separation in D18. _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-08: settings must work both with and without a stage; protection should be optional, not a prerequisite for performing.

## Review interview, 2026-10-08 (live test + grilling)

- **D53 · Show bugs before visuals:** one batch on `main` first — clip triggers no longer reset energy (D54), Escape is keyboard-locked on the fullscreen stage and losing fullscreen never shows UI on the venue screen (the warning appears in the control window), drafts leave the autopilot pool (D55), the first click that starts the local demo does not pause autopilot, an explicit **Add missing families** action puts absent families on the lab page without touching existing clips. _Why:_ live test found these on every show path.
- **D54 · Energy is show state:** the energy fader and autopilot's build/breakdown/drop moves persist across clip changes; a manual trigger keeps the current energy, and a clip's stored energy is only the starting value of a fresh show. Supersedes "clips store a base energy" in D27. _Why:_ firing a clip reset energy to 0.5, undoing every autopilot energy move; auto-flash (> 0.8) never fired in default play.
- **D55 · Lab page:** only gated families go into the show pages autopilot plays; drafts live on a lab page that autopilot skips. Supersedes "every family on page 1" in D49. _Why:_ restores D47; unreviewed looks stay off the venue screen.
- **D56 · Shared visual layer before more families:** float (`RGBA16F`) picture path — slot visuals, bloom, echo and the mix; simulation state stays `RGBA8`; `RGBA8` fallback when float targets are unavailable; ships only within the 34 ms p99 gate. Bloom, echo, chroma, grain and vignette follow energy curves; the set-wide values become ceilings; flashes stay behind the limiter. _Why:_ one palette, one transition and 8-bit banding were the largest cross-family weaknesses; fixing them lifts every family at once.
- **D57 · Palette library:** about 24 curated, mood-tagged palettes referenced by id; hand-set clip colours stay as "custom"; the hue fader stays. Pages carry a mood; autopilot steps palettes every 2–4 phrases and jumps to a contrasting palette on drops; manual input wins. Claude drafts and renders per-palette sheets across the gated families; the owner keeps or rejects. _Why:_ all default clips shared one lime/blue/pink palette.
- **D58 · Transitions:** eased crossfade, cut on the downbeat, noise dissolve and feedback melt; a clip may set its own. Autopilot picks by context — melt or dissolve in breakdowns, crossfade otherwise, and on drops the shortest smooth transition (one-bar melt). Hard cuts are manual/per-clip only, keeping D50's "autopilot changes are always smooth". _Why:_ the linear crossfade was the only transition; D50 (owner, same day) forbids autopilot cuts, so the interview's "cut on drops" applies only to manual clips.
- **D59 · Set format v4:** palettes by id, per-clip transition and energy-driven post land in `phosphor-set-v4` with a tested lossless v3 migration. _Why:_ one format bump for all set-visible changes. _Amended 2026-10-08:_ v4 ships first with palettes and page moods (step 2); per-clip transition and post fields arrive later as optional, defaulted v4 fields. Before they ship, a set saved by a newer release and opened on an older one would lose them, so all show laptops run the same release (the updater already enforces one release per browser).
- **D60 · Structural energy gate:** the family gate adds an automated minimum structural difference between energy 0.1 and 0.9 (density, motion, detail — not brightness), applied retroactively to the six gated families; a failing gated family stays playable until its rework passes. Pulse becomes strobe geometry (shape count, subdivision and motion scale with energy, kick cuts); Interference becomes layered moiré with depth (sources, frequency and colour separation scale with energy). _Why:_ at energy 1.0, Cathedral looked like mid energy and Pulse was three stripes.
- **D61 · Audio feature bus:** shared low/mid/high envelopes, onset, spectral flux and a hit event; each family maps at most three hero parameters, smoothed and capped, declared in scene metadata and checked by the gate; post may follow the low band; without beat lock everything eases back to the clock kick. _Why:_ four gated families ignored the music entirely.
- **D62 · Headliners:** Fractal Flight and Cathedral are the peak-time headliners: distance fog, in-scattered glow, ambient occlusion, low-band-driven emissive accents and energy-driven camera motion, inside the 34 ms gate. _Why:_ strongest on the live stage, both gated, both raymarched.
- **D63 · Evidence without owner mixes:** the owner provides no mixes. Beat tracking is measured on Creative-Commons techno/house DJ mixes sourced by Claude and kept outside the repo, plus `songs/`; if it misses the D24 targets, automatic tracking is off by default and the show runs tap-first. First 8-hour external-display run by 2026-10-31 on the demo pulse; a USB-interface line check with any line source before the freeze, a real mixer at the venue soundcheck; blackout latency measured at the output by screen capture, with one phone slow-motion cross-check. Each verified platform step is pushed and redeployed to Vercel; separate demo polish stays cut. Weekly sign-off uses short recordings per type at energy 0.1/0.5/0.9. Supersedes the owner-mix requirement in D24 and "further demo work" in D35. _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-08.
- **D64 · Stage ownership by window, not status cadence:** while the control knows an open stage window, performer input always goes to the stage, even if its status reports stall; local preview performance resumes only when the stage window is actually closed. _Why:_ a 2-second status gap (GPU stall) silently routed Escape and other keys to the control preview instead of the venue screen.

## Owner direction, 2026-10-09

- **D65 · Catalog instead of the Lab page:** one catalog lists every authored look of every family (`catalog.mjs`, data-driven from `scenes.mjs`), each with a lazily rendered thumbnail, family and type, **Play** and **To slot**. The owner rates looks 0–10 in half stars; the catalog sorts rated looks first (best first), then unrated, then looks rated 0; ties keep family number and preset order. Ratings live in the set as an optional v4 field `ratings` (keyed `<sceneId>:<presetName>`; malformed entries are dropped, not fatal; ratings of unknown looks are kept) and may be changed under **Lock settings**. The order stays put under the pointer and re-sorts when the pointer or focus leaves the list (or on **Sort**), keeping the just-rated card in view. A new set fills all eight pages with every look, types first and round-robin across families (looks beyond 256 slots stay in the catalog), all autopilot-allowed; an older set's page 8 named "Lab" becomes "Page 8" with autopilot allowed, once, on load or import. **Add missing families** is gone. `GATED` stays for tooling but no longer decides what plays. Thumbnails come from one extra 192×108 engine, one look at a time in idle time with at most one batch of GPU work in flight, paused during transitions and in hidden tabs; a family that fails to compile shows a palette swatch. Supersedes D55 and the Lab and Add-missing-families parts of D53. _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-09: "I don't want the Lab any more. There should be one big catalog for all looks … rate them with stars from 0–10 … sorted by rating."
- **D66 · Faster, rated, smoother autopilot:** autopilot's source is the catalog (default) or the current page (`autopilot.source`). From the catalog it plays unsaved clips (page/slot −1, keeping the show palette and energy) picked by rating weight (unrated counts as 5, 0 never plays), avoiding the last 24 looks; from a page, random picks weigh the rating of the clip's authored look (same family and preset name), else 5, avoiding the last six. Random mode plays each clip a uniformly random 8, 12 or 16 bars; in-order mode keeps `everyBars` (8/12/16/32/64, default 16 for new sets) and walks the page, or the catalog best-rated first. **Next** (button, Shift+Space, MIDI `next`) plays the next pick on the next beat with the regular fade, restarts the clip duration, is not a performer takeover and also works with autopilot off; Space alone stays tap. Regular changes fade over 12 beats (three bars) instead of 8; random mode mixes crossfade 60 %, dissolve 20 % and melt 20 %; breakdowns keep melt/dissolve, drops their four-beat melt; crossfade and dissolve ease with smootherstep. A simulation family's warm-up holds the transition at zero, so it never pops in. Amends D15 (interval, history, page-only pool), D50 (two-bar crossfades) and D58 (crossfade-only regular changes). _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-09: shorter intervals, smoother transitions, random durations, and a button to skip to the next random look.
- **D67 · Resolution:** the control's local preview resolution is a per-viewer choice (480×270 / 960×540 default / 1280×720 / 1920×1080, kept in local storage); while a stage is live the monitor shows the stage's own stream, so the choice is disabled and the editor renders at 480×270 to leave the GPU to the stage. The stage defaults to native resolution up to 4K (pixel budget 8.3 MP, "Native (up to 4K)"); sets saved before the catalog with the old 2.1 MP default move to native once on load. The governor still steps down under sustained slow frames (D45), 8.3 → 2.1 → 1 → 0.5 MP. Amends the ≈2.1 MP budget of D7. _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-09: "The preview always runs low-res and the stage high-res … the stage should always have the highest resolution."
- **D68 · The catalog is the instrument; no pages:** the 8 grid pages are gone. Everything lives in one catalog: every authored look plus the owner's **own looks** (saved edits and variations, ids `own-…`), each with a **favourite** heart and a 0–10 half-star rating. The catalog sorts favourites first, then by rating (rated, unrated, then 0), then family and preset order. The 32 grid keys (1–8, Q–I, A–K, Y–comma; MIDI `slot.0–31`) play the **first 32 cards of the current catalog view**: search or filter, and the keys play what is on screen. The control stores them in the set (`keys`), so the stage plays them without the control and after a reload. A key press fires its look on the next beat with a four-beat fade; catalog **Play** fires at once; both adopt the look's palette. Autopilot plays from the whole catalog by rating (0 never; unrated as 5). It can be limited to favourites (**♥ only**) and/or a minimum rating, and falls back to every playable look rather than standing still. A small pool avoids recent looks while keeping more than half of it to choose from. The look editor never changes an authored look: **Save as own look** creates one, **Save** and **Delete** act on own looks only. Set format v5 (`phosphor-set-v5`): `looks`, `ratings`, `favorites`, `keys`, a set-wide palette `mood` (was per page), `autopilot.favoritesOnly` and `autopilot.minRating`; no pages, no per-clip energy/fade/quantize/autopilot flags. v4 → v5 migrates each page clip that differs from its authored look (family, preset, seed or parameters) into an own look, keeping name, colours and transition, plus ratings and the first page's mood. Local saves move to `phosphor-set-v5`; the v4 entry stays as a backup. Shift+1–8 no longer does anything. Supersedes D11's pages, D13's page keys, D19's clip fields, the page parts of D15, D57 (page mood → set mood), D65 (pages filled with every look, **To slot**) and D66 (page source). _Why:_ owner direction 2026-10-09: "No eight pages any more, only one catalog with everything in it, where you can rate and favourite."
- **D69 · Editorial order, most interesting first:** looks with the same rating (before any rating: all of them) follow an editorial order in `catalog-curated.mjs` instead of family number. Each family has a strength (signature algorithm, club impact, how trippy) and its looks a best-first order. A look scores strength − 1.5 × its rank in the family, so the strongest families lead and every family shows its best look within the first two looks per family. Own looks follow the authored ones. With nothing rated, the catalog, the 32 grid keys and the in-order autopilot walk all start with Mandelbrot Seahorse Valley, Menger Long Corridor, Prismatic Nave, Gosper Barrage, Swirl Flame and Lorenz Butterfly. Ratings and favourites still win over it (D68). The list is generated by `scripts/curate.mjs` from a table of family strengths and best-first look orders; every new look goes into that table, and a test checks that the list covers every authored look exactly once. _Why:_ owner request 2026-10-09: "sort all looks in the order you find most interesting, best first."
