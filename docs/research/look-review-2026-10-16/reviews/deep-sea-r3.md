Keep darkness, but give the sparse state scale and a deliberate spatial composition.
2. **Replace the clip-art reef.** In stills 9–12, especially the foreground fans in 10–11, coral reads as repeated planar branching symbols and sea pens as evenly stamped leaves. Show a compact leading cohort progressing from reef polyps into nearby plankton and bell canals, followed by a dim wake.
