## Must fix for ≥ 8

1. **Make the relay's race the visible event, not just the cap repaint.** Where: rhythm frames 4–6, 12–14 and 17–20.
   - Now: the travelling head is a few pixels near the frame edge or hidden behind the hero cap.
   - Instead: route each relay along a thick foreground cord in the lower third, crossing at least a quarter of the frame width. Give it a hot pink head and a fading wake, and split it visibly at one junction into two branches. One branch should climb a stem.
   - On arrival (frames 7, 16, 21): light the gills from inside, with the colour fading towards the rim. Do not flood the whole cap with flat magenta, which currently reads as a colour swap.
2. **Recompose the held peak.** Where: rhythm frames 1–24.
   - Now: one dark-navy foreground cap fills 30–45 % of the frame. As the camera drifts, it becomes a cropped blank slab on the left edge (frames 13–24) and hides both the network and the relay route.
   - Instead: put the hero cap on a third with its glowing underside towards the camera, and open the lower third onto bright cords feeding into the stem bases. Clamp the drift so no occluder parks on an edge.
3. **Fruiting-body material.**
   - Where: sheet 10–12 and the background stacks in the rhythm strip.
   - Now: olive caps read as stacked lily pads or leaves. Blue caps read as matte plastic umbrellas with flat navy tops. Stems are thin dark lines that vanish into black, with no visible link to the network.
   - Instead: fleshier convex caps with a translucent subsurface rim and varied cap tones (move the olive towards a luminous chartreuse rather than muddy olive). Make stems pale and translucent, glowing at the base where the cords enter.
4. **Make the colony the light source.** Where: sheet 9–12 and the whole rhythm strip.
   - Now: the fruiting stage is dimmer and greyer than the ground network. The mushrooms look like lit props under blue-grey fog, and the empty navy band in the top 15 % adds nothing.
   - Instead: gill glow should spill onto the floor and nearby stems, and spore haze should be lit locally around the colony. Do this through local light and falloff, not global exposure.
5. **Breakdown and network hierarchy.**
   - Where: sheet 1–4. Now: the focal junction is a small closed loop with a hook, more like a calligraphic symbol than a colony hub, and the frame is still over 60 % black with thin threads of similar width. Instead: a dense fan of thick, tapering cords radiating from the hub, with fuzzy hyphal margins and blurred near-camera fibres for depth.
   - Where: sheet 5–8. Now: lime spark nodes are spread evenly across the plane. Instead: thin them so that 2–3 hubs dominate.
   - Also remove the stray orange blob at left-centre in sheet 7.
