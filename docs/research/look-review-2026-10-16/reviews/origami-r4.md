## Must fix for ≥ 8

1. **Make the quiet composition a space, not three samples on black.** Sheet 1–5: similarly sized, horizontally stacked strips float above heavy black shadows; distant strips barely register. Use a stronger near/far scale hierarchy, an oblique foreground fold and readable atmospheric separation while preserving breakdown darkness.
2. **Give the peak a genuinely developed silhouette.** Sheet 8–12: two broad curled bands dominate an otherwise empty stage; the result resembles a segmented conveyor sculpture rather than an origami storm. Develop the existing sheets into nested, articulated folds with a clear hero plane and subordinate swept layers—not disconnected confetti.
3. **Replace rigid armour-like faceting with convincing paper.** Sheet 9–11 and `peak-large.png`: thick-looking olive panels and repeated triangular shading read as low-poly plastic. Show thin cut edges, distinct mountain/valley creases, softer face gradients and restrained transmitted light through backlit paper.
4. **Restore colour and focal hierarchy.** Peak faces are mostly dull olive and muted blue; no rare accent commands attention. Keep saturated mid-tones in the lit folds, darken recessed faces, and reserve a small bright accent for the active crease instead of broadly whitening the material.
5. **Make the groove readable between the big snaps.** Rhythm 6–15 and 18–24 change little at sheet scale despite the full grid. Give edge-flash a localized travelling catchlight and fold-breath a readable hinge release; let each snap visibly propagate and settle. The auxiliary 30-fps strip confirms folding, but not continuous-motion smoothness or live limiter compliance.
