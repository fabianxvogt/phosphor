## Must fix for ≥ 8

1. **Light the near and mid ground with material, not with a horizon halo.** Where: every rhythm frame and sheet 9–12. All the luminous crowns sit in a strip 20–35 % from the top, above a near-black carpet of outlined cups. Instead: give the near crowns subsurface transmission, so light passes through the petal thickness and the veins glow from the base outward. Use a near-to-far exposure gradient, frame the hero crowns in the middle third, and drop the halo around the top-left crown.
2. **Bud material in stills 1–7 reads as striped glass eggs or paper lanterns** (failure patterns 2 and 8). Every bud has the same pale outline and evenly spaced meridian lines. Instead: tapered buds wrapped in overlapping sepals, with varied tilt and size, a green-to-indigo gradient from base to tip, and light leaking from the petal seams. Seven of the twelve sheet stills are this state, so it weighs heavily on L.
3. **Make both pollen releases read at a glance.** Rhythm 15–17: the second release is a small, half-hidden spray at mid-distance. Instead: each pollen-rising picks a crown in the near or mid ground that is at least 15 % of frame width, and alternates left and right. Pollen should arc up past the crowns into the sky. Also cup and shade the flat lavender petal in sheet 10, as the crimson petals in the pollen detail already are.
4. **Break the single-hue palette.** Sheet 8–12 and the rhythm strip are uniformly indigo-blue, plus a dull yellow-green haze under the horizon crowns that reads like lit lawn. Instead: a saturated spread of violet, cobalt and teal by species or depth. Keep pink as the rare accent and pull the green haze down to small specks at the flower bases.
5. **Make the vein pulse carry the driving groove.** Rhythm 4, 7, 11 and 23: the gold vein flecks are tiny and sit on distant petals. Frames 18–21 barely change. Instead: on vein-pulse hits, run light from the stem up through the hero crown's veins and fade it within the half-beat, so the 6.5 hits/bar groove is visible on the dominant flower.
