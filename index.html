Local autosave

Sets and lineages remain on this device. Recording streams to disk where supported; memory fallback stops at 64 MB. Frame export renders 120 deterministic 720p PNGs at 30 fps without audio. No photosensitivity certification; no strobe feature is included.

PHOSPHOR · local-first desktop performance instrument · WebGL2 / Chromium recommended · hardware rehearsal required before a show

Performing Phosphor

1. Choose a family and authored preset. Drag the preview to paint.

2. Start demo audio or select a local line-in; map bands to controls.

3. Capture and arrange cues, then open clean output on your projector.

4. Play the score, GO manually, or enable Director. Save/export before your show.
